Los prompts completos utilizados son:

## pencil.png

Full bleed artwork, no frame, no text, no lettering, no watermark, no interface. The central 60% and upper half must stay exceptionally quiet with abundant negative space for readable app text. Low contrast details and few elements; refined intentional handmade artwork, not a generic gradient. Primary request: an exquisite minimal graphite pencil drawing on warm ivory drawing paper. A few fine, imperfect pencil contours suggest distant mountains along the bottom quarter. At the lower right, a tiny solitary pine drawn with delicate graphite hatching, barely 10 percent of image height. Subtle real paper tooth, faint erased construction lines near the mountain contours only. Broad unmarked cream sky. Restrained gray pencil, nuanced and poetic, quiet Japanese sketchbook sensibility. No colored paint, no big dark foreground.
